Payment and cancellation

To reserve your stay, a 25% deposit is taken upon booking with the remainder due at least 30 days before arrival. A security deposit is required before arriving to the property and is completely refunded within one week of your departure. Payment can be made by check, PayPal, electronic transfer, or Apple Pay. Credit cards are possible; any card fees are the guest's responsibility.

There is no charge for cancellations more than 60 days out. Less than 60 days out, 25% of the deposit is kept. Less than 30 days out, 50% of the deposit is kept. Cancellations made less than 14 days before arrival are non-refundable. If you need to cancel, we will try our best to fill the days you had reserved. If those days are filled, we refund the deposit in full. Extenuating circumstances can be reviewed at the discretion of the owners.
