Payment and cancellation

To reserve your stay, a 25% deposit is taken upon booking with the remainder due at least 30 days before arrival. A security deposit is required before arriving and is completely refunded within one week of your departure.

Payment can be made by check, PayPal, electronic transfer, or Apple Pay. Credit cards are possible; any card fees are the guest's responsibility.

  • More than 60 days out: no charge
  • Less than 60 days: 25% of deposit kept
  • Less than 30 days: 50% of deposit kept
  • Less than 14 days: non-refundable

If the reserved days can be filled again, the deposit is refunded in full. Extenuating circumstances can be reviewed by the owners.
